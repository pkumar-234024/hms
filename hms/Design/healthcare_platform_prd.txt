# Healthcare Platform Product Requirements

## Overview
A premium SaaS healthcare platform providing modules for Hospital Administrators, Doctors, and Patients.

## Core Modules & Screens

### 1. Public Facing
- **Landing Page**: Hero with medical imagery, feature overview, CTA.
- **About Page**: Mission, specialist showcase, achievements, testimonials.
- **Contact Page**: Contact form, emergency cards, hospital map.
- **Feedback Page**: Patient reviews, star ratings, emoji reactions.
- **Auth**: Login/Register with 2FA support and doctor-specific portals.

### 2. Super Admin Dashboard
- **Analytics**: Revenue, patient/doctor trends, activity logs.
- **Management**: Doctor CRUD, patient management, registration approval.
- **Operations**: Bed availability, emergency case tracking.

### 3. Doctor Panel
- **Dashboard**: Today's appointments, quick-access patient search.
- **Patient Records**: Medical history, report uploads, prescription management.
- **Schedule**: Calendar management, video consultation triggers.

### 4. Patient Dashboard
- **My Health**: Medical history, report downloads, health analytics.
- **Appointments**: Multi-step booking system, upcoming visits.
- **Billing**: Payment history and insurance details.

### 5. Shared Features
- AI Health Assistant chatbot.
- Real-time notifications and emergency alert banners.
- Dark/Light mode toggle.
- Responsive sidebar and top navigation.
